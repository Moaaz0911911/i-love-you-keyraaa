A letter

Every word is yours

My Keyra,

I've tried to write this a hundred times, and every time the words feel too small for you. So I'll just say it plainly: I love you. I love you more than I know how to put on a page.

Since the 17th of July, my days have been divided into two kinds: the ones I spend with you, and the ones I spend waiting to be with you.

I love the way you say my name. I love how you look when you're pretending you're not smiling. I love that you make me feel like the luckiest person alive without even trying.

You are my calm and my favorite chaos. You are every good thing I used to hope for, and so many things I never knew to ask for.

If I could, I would give you the sky, every star in it, and then I'd tell you that none of them shine like you do.

Thank you for choosing me, and for loving me exactly as I am. I promise to love you gently, to choose you every day, and to hold your hand through every season we get.

Forever yours,

— AZZY
